Common Questions

Selling to Us — Frequently Asked

Do you buy from shops or dealers, or just individual collectors?

Both — we work with individual collectors as well as other shops and dealers looking to move sealed product or full collections.

Do I have to be local to Plantation, FL to sell to you?

We primarily work with local sellers so we can take a look at product in person, but send over the form either way — we're happy to talk through what you have.

How fast will I hear back after I submit the form?

We review submissions quickly and aim to get back to you within a day or two — often sooner.

Do you buy singles or graded slabs, or only sealed product?

Our main focus is sealed product and full collections, but we'll take a look at singles and graded slabs too — just tell us what you have.
