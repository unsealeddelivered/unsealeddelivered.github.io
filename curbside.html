Curbside Pickup

Under 21? Running in and out? Either way — tell us what you want and where you're parked, and we'll bring it straight to your car.

How Curbside Pickup Works

📝 1. Tell Us What You Want

Fill out the form with what you're looking for and a bit about your vehicle so we know who we're looking for.

📞 2. We Confirm & Get It Ready

We'll text or call to confirm it's in stock and get your order pulled and ready to go.

🚗 3. Pull Up, We'll Bring It Out

Park out front and let us know you've arrived — we'll walk your order straight out to the car.

🔞 Heads Up

Unsealed & Delivered Co. operates out of the back room of the shop up front, and guests under 21 aren't able to come inside per store policy. Curbside pickup is open to everyone — no age restriction on ordering, just on stepping inside.
