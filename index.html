Our Story

Built on Kitchen-Table Trades

Unsealed & Delivered Co. started the way a lot of great collections do — with a family that couldn't stop trading, opening, and hunting for the next great pull.

Today that same passion runs our storefront in Plantation, where we stock the sealed product, sports cards, and toys we'd want to find ourselves.
