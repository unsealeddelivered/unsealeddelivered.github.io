About Unsealed & Delivered Co. — Family-Owned Hobby Shop in Plantation, FL

Family Owned. Hobby Obsessed.

Unsealed & Delivered Co. is a small, family-run shop in Plantation, FL, built by collectors who couldn't stay away from the hobby.

Our Story

From the Kitchen Table to a Storefront

Like a lot of shops in this hobby, ours started small — trading, opening packs, and chasing that next great pull as a family. What began as a shared love for TCGs, sports cards, and collectibles turned into something we wanted to share with our community.

Today, Unsealed & Delivered Co. is our brick-and-mortar home in Plantation, Florida, where we stock sealed, mint product and the toys that go along with it — from booster boxes to Squishmallows.

  • ✓ Family owned and operated, start to finish
  • ✓ Every product sealed and verified mint
  • ✓ Rooted in the Plantation community
